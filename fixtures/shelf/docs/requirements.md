# Shelf: requirements

## Must

- A reader signs in, lists books to lend, and borrows books from neighbours.
- "Nearby" shows books within walking distance. Location is used only while the reader is searching, and never leaves the phone except as a rough area (the first half of the postcode).
- A reader's saved books survive every app update.
- Sessions stay private on a shared or lost phone.
- The app works with VoiceOver and TalkBack, and respects the phone's text size.
- The app talks to the API over HTTPS only.

## Operating in

The United Kingdom, so UK GDPR applies.
