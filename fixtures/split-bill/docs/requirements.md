# Split Bill: requirements

Split Bill splits a restaurant bill between the people at the table, to the cent.

## Must

- Work in whole cents, so no share has a fraction of a cent.
- Make the shares add up to exactly the bill: no cent lost or invented.
- Reject input that can't be split: a negative or fractional amount, or nobody to pay.

## Backlog

- An optional tip, as a percentage of the bill.
- Uneven splits, where some people pay for more than others.
