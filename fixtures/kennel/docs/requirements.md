# Kennel: requirements

Dog owners book stays at the kennel, and the staff look after the dogs.

## Must

- An owner signs in and books a stay for their dog: the dog's name and the dates.
- An owner sees only their own bookings.
- An owner pays a deposit by card when booking. The payment provider takes the payment; Kennel never sees card details, and marks a booking paid only when the provider says so.
- Staff post daily updates on a stay, with a photo. Owners read them.
- Before check-in, Kennel checks the dog's vaccinations with the vet records service. A dog whose vaccinations can't be confirmed isn't checked in.

## Operating in

Ireland, so the GDPR applies to owners' details and their dogs' records.
