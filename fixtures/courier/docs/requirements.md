# Courier: requirements

Customers book a courier to collect a parcel and deliver it to a recipient.

## Must

- A customer signs in and books a pickup: the parcel's weight, where to collect it, and who receives it.
- A customer sees only their own parcels.
- A customer pays by card when booking. Payment is taken by the payment provider; Courier never stores card details.
- Anyone with a parcel's tracking link can see its status, but not the recipient's details.
- The web app works with a keyboard and a screen reader.

## Operating in

The United Kingdom, so UK GDPR applies to customer and recipient details.
