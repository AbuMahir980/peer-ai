# Sprout: requirements

## Must

- Works with no connection. The device holds the only copy of a person's plants, photos and journal until sync arrives, so nothing may ever be lost: not in an update, and not when the browser runs short of space.
- Updates reach every person the next time they open the app.
- Identifying a plant sends only the photo, never where it was taken, and says so before sending.
- Advice from the identification service is shown as a suggestion, never as fact. Anything about safety for pets or children says to check with an expert.
- Works with a keyboard and a screen reader.

## Operating in

Nigeria, so the NDPA applies.
