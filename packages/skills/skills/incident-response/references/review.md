# The incident review

The incident document starts as the timeline and ends as the review. Blameless: it describes what the system allowed, not who slipped. Plain words, facts marked as known or inferred.

## Parts

1. **Summary:** what happened, who was affected, how long for, and whether it's over, in a few lines.
2. **Impact:** how many people, what they saw, and what happened to their data or money. Anything that couldn't be recovered.
3. **Timeline:** each time, with its time zone, what was seen and what was done.
4. **Cause:** the change or condition that explains everything seen.
5. **Why it wasn't caught:** the test, review, check or alert that would have stopped it or shown it sooner.
6. **What helped, and what didn't:** in finding it, containing it and fixing it.
7. **Who was told:** inside, the people affected, and any regulator, with when, or the decision still waiting on someone qualified.
8. **Follow-ups:** each a work item, by id, with what it prevents.

## A good follow-up

- Changes the system, not a person's habits: "a test that a cancelled booking frees its slot runs on every change", not "be more careful".
- Is specific enough to finish, and says which part of the review it answers.
