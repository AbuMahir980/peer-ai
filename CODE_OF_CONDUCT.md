# Code of conduct

Peer AI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and give feedback on the work, not the person. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [@AbuMahir980](https://github.com/AbuMahir980), at [lawalqudus980@gmail.com](mailto:lawalqudus980@gmail.com). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and responds in proportion, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project.
