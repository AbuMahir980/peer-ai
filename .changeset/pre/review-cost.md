`project_map` gives each review's rough size over the whole project, `reviewSizes` (RFC 0016). For each review, it gives the files, lines and rules in its scope, and a size of small, medium or large, with the token range that means. Before a whole-project review starts, the review skill tells the person, and asks whether to run it whole, for one part, or not now.
