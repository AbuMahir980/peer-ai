Add 78 rules across ten more domains: frontend, design and accessibility, backend, API design, data, performance, reliability, system design, testing, and privacy and compliance, plus a money rule that keeps full card numbers and security codes off your servers. Accessibility rules cite the WCAG 2.2 success criterion they come from, and a test checks every WCAG citation against the numbers and levels taken from the W3C, as it already did for OWASP ASVS. Several rules come from what the practice projects showed: stored data must never be lost in a change, information is never shown by colour alone, every call to another service has a timeout, and personal data is collected only at the precision needed.
