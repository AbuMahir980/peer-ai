Add the tech-debt-triage skill. It finds what makes a codebase slow or risky to change, in the code, the tests, the dependencies, the history and how it runs; raises anything hurting people now as a fix first, not as debt; ranks the rest by what it costs and risks against what fixing takes; and turns the top of the list into work items with goals and acceptance criteria, keeping the rest in a register. It changes no code.
