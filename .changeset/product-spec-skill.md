Add the product-spec skill. It writes one feature's spec before it's built: who can do what, on which records; each journey, the ones that go wrong included; each screen in its loading, empty, error and offline states; acceptance criteria; edge cases such as time zones and two people at once; and why each personal field is needed. Where the requirements don't decide something, it asks, or marks its recommendation as proposed.
