Add the issue-planning skill, the first work skill. It turns a spec, a design, the requirements or the project's gaps into small work items that each ship on their own, sliced through the layers rather than by layer. Each carries its plan (RFC 0005): a goal, acceptance criteria taken from the spec, its sources, and only the dependencies that are real. It reads open items first so nothing is planned twice, offers matching tracker issues only when the person agrees, and ends by checking the order with `next_work` and `peer-ai check`.
