Add the accessibility-review skill. It checks every screen, control, field, message and moving part against the eleven accessibility rules, each from WCAG 2.2: names and labels, keyboard use and visible focus, alternatives to dragging, never colour alone, contrast worked out as a ratio from the colour values, text that grows, touch targets, reduced motion, and error messages that say what to do. What needs the running app is marked not checked, with what to test by hand.
