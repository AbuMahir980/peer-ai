Add the compliance-review skill. It follows every personal field and every card payment from where it's collected to everywhere it goes (logs, outside services, AI models, URLs, other apps) and checks each against the privacy rules: only what's needed, only where people were told and consented, hidden photo details removed, nothing in logs or URLs, a set time it's kept, and card details never on your own servers. It names the law a finding matters under as a pointer, leaves legal questions to someone qualified, and offers to save the data inventory it builds.
