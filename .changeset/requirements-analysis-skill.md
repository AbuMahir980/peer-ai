Add the requirements-analysis skill, the first document skill. It writes or updates a product's requirements from a brief, a request or the code: the people and their problems, the needs behind the features, every feature's acceptance criteria, the scope, and the data and laws that switch on rules. Every statement says where it came from, and what nobody has said becomes a question rather than a guess. It ends by checking the document with `check_document`.
